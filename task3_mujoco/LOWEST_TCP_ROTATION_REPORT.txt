LOWEST-POINT TCP ROTATION UPDATE
===============================

Both FR3 arms now rotate about a TCP placed at the midpoint of the lowest
surfaces of the closed Robotiq fingertip collision meshes.

TCP in gripper-base coordinates:
  x =  0.000000000 m
  y = -0.000375000 m
  z =  0.163052783 m

Equivalent TCP position in the FR3 link8 frame:
  x = -0.000265165 m
  y = -0.000265165 m
  z =  0.163052783 m

Implementation:
- Added left_arm_control_tcp_pivot and right_arm_control_tcp_pivot sites.
- Moved both control TCP bodies from the flange origin to the lower gripper tip.
- Changed inverse kinematics from mj_jacBody to mj_jacSite.
- Rotation mode stores and holds the TCP world position.
- Added bounded linear pivot correction during rotation.
- Added separate lower IK damping of 0.001 during rotation.
- Translation behavior, gripper preload, spoon collision, and solver parameters
  remain unchanged.
